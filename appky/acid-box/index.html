Acid Box

Acid bass synth, step sequencer and techno drums

Press Play, then tap a Groove: it loads a whole sound at once. Or build your own: pick a bass Patch and a Techno beat, then turn Cutoff, Reso and Env Mod while it runs. Beats set their own tempo. Perform buttons are for live tricks: hold Stutter or Drop, tap Fill or Riser. Scenes A and B hold two patterns; Chain switches between them. Keys: Space play, 1 and 2 stutter, 3 brake, ` drop, F fill, T riser, R roll, Z–M stabs. Save your own sounds into the 6 slots.
